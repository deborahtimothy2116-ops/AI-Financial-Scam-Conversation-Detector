Frequently Asked Intelligence Questions

ScamShield AI evaluated this pitch against hundreds of confirmed "Prepaid Task" scams logged by the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C). These schemes follow a standardized playbook:

  • They offer disproportionate payouts (e.g., ₹3,000–₹8,000 daily) for unskilled tasks such as reviewing products, liking YouTube videos, or rating Google Maps locations.
  • They shift targets from regulated channels like WhatsApp to end-to-end encrypted Telegram channels to evade bulk-spam filters and telephone number blacklists.
  • They offer an instant small deposit ("₹500 welcome bonus") to induce psychological commitment before requesting larger "unlock deposits" or "cryptocurrency task staking".

Plain Language Advice: Never pay money to receive wages. An employer paying ₹8,000/day for part-time review work would be paying ₹2.4 Lakhs/month—far above realistic market value for entry-level data tasks.

If you have contacted the handle, immediately archive and block the user on Telegram. Do not submit your UPI ID, bank account details, Aadhaar card, or debit card statements. If you already received a nominal "bonus" in your bank account, contact your bank immediately to flag the inbound transaction to prevent your account from being frozen during cyber-fraud cleanups.
